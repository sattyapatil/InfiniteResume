## Who Is Infinite Resume For?

One of the most common misconceptions about AI resume builders is that they are designed primarily for students and entry-level job seekers. Infinite Resume was built to serve every career level.

### Executives, Directors, and Senior Management

The AI Resume Doctor specializes in "Brevity Scoring," a feature specifically designed to help senior leaders condense decades of complex career history into high-impact, ATS-optimized pages without losing strategic depth. The platform includes dedicated Executive templates that perfectly structure board-level achievements, P&L responsibilities, and organizational transformations.

### Experienced Professionals and Career Switchers

For mid-career professionals looking to pivot industries or advance to the next level, the Job Match & Tailor feature analyzes how well your resume matches a role's requirements, identifies missing keywords, and one-click optimizes your resume with tailored phrasing and matched skills.

### Fresh Graduates and Entry-Level Candidates

The AI Resume Import feature allows first-time resume creators to simply describe their experience in plain English. The AI Content Writer then transforms these descriptions into professionally structured, metric-driven bullet points. Minimalistic and Classic ATS templates focus on education, skills, and projects.

### Freelancers, Consultants, and Gig Workers

The platform supports project-based resume structures, portfolio sections, and flexible formatting that adapts to non-traditional career paths.

## The Technology: Typst + WebAssembly

Most resume builders (Zety, Resume.io, Canva) use a headless browser to "print" HTML to PDF. This approach has critical ATS compatibility issues:

- Text becomes SVG vectors → ATS parsers see nothing
- Invisible whitespace breaks character spacing
- Font ligatures ("fi", "fl") become unrecognizable glyphs
- File sizes balloon to 500KB+ with embedded fonts

### How Infinite Resume Solves This

Infinite Resume uses [Typst](https://typst.app), a next-generation typesetting system (think LaTeX, but modern and fast), compiled to [WebAssembly](https://webassembly.org) and running entirely in the user's browser:

- **Native text layer** with correct Unicode mappings → every character is individually addressable by ATS parsers
- **Sub-500ms compilation** → real-time preview updates as you type
- **50–150KB file sizes** → lean, optimized PDFs
- **Client-side rendering** → your resume data never leaves your browser
- **Guaranteed ATS parsing** by Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, Lever, and all major enterprise hiring platforms

This is a fundamental architectural advantage over any builder using HTML-to-PDF conversion.
