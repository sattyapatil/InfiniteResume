## Technology

### What technology powers the PDF engine?

Infinite Resume uses **Typst**, a next-generation typesetting system compiled to **WebAssembly**. Unlike HTML-to-PDF tools (used by Zety, Resume.io, Canva), Typst produces PDFs with a native text layer — meaning every character is individually addressable by ATS parsers. This guarantees flawless parsing by Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, Lever, and all major enterprise ATS platforms.

### What AI model does Infinite Resume use?

The platform is powered by **Google Gemini AI** for all content generation, resume analysis, and optimization features.

### Is my data safe?

Yes. Resume PDF generation runs entirely client-side via WebAssembly — your resume content never leaves your browser during the PDF creation process. AI-powered features send data to Google Gemini for processing, but no resume data is stored on third-party AI servers after processing is complete.

## ATS & Resume Quality

### Will my resume pass ATS systems?

Yes. The Typst PDF engine produces documents with native text layers and correct Unicode mappings. Every character is individually addressable by ATS parsers. The platform is tested and verified to parse correctly with Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, Lever, iCIMS, and all major enterprise Applicant Tracking Systems.

### How is this different from Zety, Resume.io, or Canva?

Three critical differences:

1. **Free PDF downloads** — Zety and Resume.io require paid subscriptions to download PDFs. Infinite Resume is free forever.
2. **No watermarks** — Competitors add watermarks to free-tier downloads. Infinite Resume never adds watermarks.
3. **Superior PDF technology** — Competitors use HTML-to-PDF (headless browser rendering), which breaks ATS parsing. Infinite Resume uses Typst + WebAssembly for pixel-perfect, ATS-guaranteed output.

### What ATS systems does Infinite Resume work with?

All of them. The Typst PDF engine produces documents with industry-standard text encoding that is compatible with every major ATS platform, including: Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, Lever, iCIMS, BambooHR, JazzHR, Jobvite, SmartRecruiters, Breezy HR, and others.
